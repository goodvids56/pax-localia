# Pax Localia bundled data

## Schematic atomic-region maps

The `border-crisis-v1`, `july-crisis-v1`, and `orpheus-reach-v1` GeoJSON
datasets are original schematic geometry created for Pax Localia. They are
dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. They do not claim survey-level
accuracy and are intentionally small enough for deterministic tests and local
models.

## Seed scenario text and data

“The Selene Border Crisis” and “Signals Across Orpheus Reach” are original
fictional works by Pax Localia contributors, licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0.

“The July Crisis: Balkan Ultimatum” is an original scenario synthesis using
broad historical facts and the public-domain Austro-Hungarian ultimatum of 23
July 1914. The scenario uses a deliberately schematic map and does not
represent precise 1914 borders. Original scenario wording and data are licensed
CC-BY-SA-4.0.
